## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an editorial boutique maternity and perinatal atmosphere, blending high-end clinical diligence with warm, empathetic maternal wellness. It bridges the precision of specialized obstetric care with the reassuring, tactile elegance of an editorial wellness sanctuary.

### Emotional Response
- **Serenity & Trust:** Soft, mineral rose tones and clean crystalline structures remove the anxiety typically associated with clinical interfaces.
- **Dignified Care:** Typographic expression and rhythmic layouts convey premium care, honoring both maternal patience and clinical rigor.
- **Tactile Clarity:** Segmented layouts, pill-shaped tactile controls, and soft translucency provide effortless scanability for critical perinatal data.

### Aesthetic Movement
An amalgamation of **Editorial Elegance** and **Tactile Glassmorphism**. Surfaces feature translucent rose quartz materials, delicate inner boundary borders, and soft pillular geometry, grounded by disciplined grid alignments and structured typographic hierarchies.

## Colors

The chromatic palette centers around warm rose quartz minerals, supported by clinical grounding accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#E11D48`):** Deep Rose Quartz. Expresses authority, maternal health focus, and decisive interactive states. Used for primary calls-to-action, key metrics, and focused segment controls.
- **Secondary (`#FDA4AF`):** Soft Petal Blush. Provides gentle warmth, non-intrusive container backgrounds, and highlight fills.
- **Tertiary (`#0F766E`):** Clinical Pine / Emerald. Utilized as an anchor for clinical telemetry, stable physiological states, and multi-disciplinary validation.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - Base Surface Canvas: `#FFF5F5` to `#FFF1F2` (Rose Quartz tint).
  - Surface Containers: Pure `#FFFFFF` with high-translucency rose undertones.
  - Text Primary: `#4C0519` (Deep Berry Black) to ensure AA/AAA contrast ratios while avoiding sterile neutral blacks.
  - Text Secondary: `#881337` with 70% opacity.
  - Borders & Insets: `#FFE4E6` with hairline translucency.

### Clinical Status Accents
- **Urgent / Maternal Contraction Peak:** `#BE123C` (Ruby).
- **Active Telemetry / Stable:** `#0F766E` (Deep Teal).
- **Observation / Advisory:** `#D97706` (Warm Ochre).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial, sculptured warmth of **Epilogue** for display headings with the ergonomic readability of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for real-time maternal vitals, diagnostics, and patient narrative notes.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Headlines (`Epilogue`):** Distinctive, geometric curves convey care with artistic character. Used selectively for stage titles (e.g., "Trimester Assessment", "Labor Progression"), major metric callouts, and greeting banners.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, open apertures that maintain crisp legibility on handheld clinical tablets and mobile devices. Used for all diagnostic logging, clinical records, and physiological telemetry labels.
- **Uppercase Micro-labels:** All `label-sm` elements must use upper case with expanded letter spacing (`+0.03em`) for physiological metrics (e.g., `BPM`, `FHR`, `TOCO`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an adaptable fluid grid, utilizing a rhythmic 8pt spatial baseline with 4pt micro-steps for tight clinical metrics.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack. Outer margins are `1rem` (16px), allowing cards maximum horizontal room while avoiding screen edge clipping. 
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Gutters and margins expand to `1.5rem` (24px) for split-view patient monitoring and timeline charting side-by-side.
- **Desktop (>= 1025px):** 12-column layout. Margin expands to `3rem` (48px) with a centered maximum layout container of `1280px` to maintain focused ergonomic eye-tracking for midwives and physicians.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through translucent rose quartz surfaces, diffuse ambient undertones, and delicate hairline inner rims rather than aggressive drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** Soft matte blush foundation (`#FFF5F5`).
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Trays):** Background `#FFFFFF` with 88% opacity, coupled with a backdrop blur of `24px`. Rimmed with an inner stroke of `1px` solid `#FFE4E6` to simulate polished quartz edges. Shadow is diffused: `0 8px 32px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Trays & Overlays):** Background `#FFFFFF` with 96% opacity, backdrop blur `32px`, and a duo-tone elevation: `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02), 0 16px 40px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Critical Alerts):** Solid `#FFFFFF` card with ambient perimeter glow: `0 24px 64px rgba(76, 5, 25, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a pronounced pill-like visual language (`roundedness: 3`), providing an approachable touch-friendly surface that diminishes clinical hostility.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Micro Elements (Chips, Badges, Switches):** Fully rounded pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px).
- **Segmented Controls & Action Buttons:** Continuous pill shape (`1rem` to `1.5rem` radius based on height).
- **Small Containers & Inset Panels:** `1rem` (16px) corner radius.
- **Primary Patient Cards & Modals:** `2rem` (32px) corner radius (`rounded-lg`), mirroring smooth polished pebbles.
- **Full View Sheet Presentation:** Top corners styled with `3rem` (48px) corner radius (`rounded-xl`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped, deep rose solid fill (`#E11D48`), high-contrast white text (`Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold`), minimum touch target of 48px height. Pressed states feature a subtle inner shadow and 0.98 scale transform.
- **Secondary Button:** Translucent quartz fill (`rgba(253, 164, 175, 0.2)`), `#E11D48` text, enclosed in a 1px border of `#FDA4AF`.
- **Segmented Control Buttons:** Nested inside an encapsulated pill track (`#FFE4E6`), active item features a bright white pill container elevated with a 2px diffuse rose shadow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Vitals & Triage Badges:** Fully circular pill containers. For instance, maternal contraction monitors feature a light rose tinted background (`#FFF1F2`), an active pulsing dot indicator, and `label-sm` bold typography in `#BE123C`.
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** Height 32px, `space-sm` horizontal padding, shifting from soft muted rose quartz to filled `#E11D48` when toggled.

### Cards
- **Segmented Care Cards:** Composed with an outer card radius of 32px (`rounded-lg`). Internally divided into distinct structural compartments using subtle `1px` translucent separators (`#FFE4E6`). Top compartments host high-level patient vitals in Epilogue, while bottom compartments house medical logs and timestamp actions in Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Input Fields
- **Clinical Form Inputs:** Pill-shaped 52px height containers. Filled with `#FFFFFF` over base canvas, highlighted by a subtle `1px` border of `#FECDD3`. On focus, the field transitions to an active `#E11D48` border with a `0 0 0 4px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.1)` glow ring.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Radio Buttons:** Smooth concentric pill/circle with 24px diameter. The outer ring is `#FDA4AF`, transitioning into an `#E11D48` central dot with smooth spring animation on selection.
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded squircle (8px radius) with deep rose background fill and white vector checkmark upon activation.

### Perinatal Timeline Tracker
- Dedicated visual stream featuring connected soft nodes. Completed gestational or labor stages display filled Deep Rose nodes with white internal checkmarks; active stages pulse with a dual-ring quartz halo.
